## 1. ВАЖНО: точная операторная эквивалентность и ограничение её класса

K_phi=J(2I−S_phi−S_phi†), S_phi^N=e^{i phi}I. f_M(x)=sqrt(M²+2Mx)−M, M,J>0. ε(q)=f_M(2J(1−cos q)) is real even periodic, ε(0)=0. Signed Fourier convention j̃_r=−(1/2π)∫ε(q)cos(rq)dq. Therefore exactly

f_M(K_phi)=2 Σ_{r≥1} j̃_r[I−(S_phi^r+S_phi^{†r})/2].

Uniform absolute convergence; this is equality of matrices/functions on the same twisted Hilbert space for every phi, not merely equality of eigenvalues. All static free spectra, spectral projectors, coherently measured operators with the same preparation/probe, Gibbs traces and free evolution coincide. No free-sector experiment can distinguish two *exactly identical* operators in the same operational dictionary. It does NOT imply all long-range Hamiltonians equal f(K). After overall J scale the f-family is one-parameter (M/J). Known optical signed couplings distinguish these particular restricted families when harmonics are independently recovered; arbitrary effective couplings can mimic f exactly. Under fixed phase alone there is aliasing, since S^{r+mN}=e^{im phi}S^r. Full mode-identified phase sweep with a common reference can recover more harmonics; even complete identification of H does not identify its microscopic origin.

Analytic strengthening of neighbor's sign observation: use Bernstein representation f_M(x)=sqrt(M/(2π))∫_0^∞(1−e^{−sx})e^{−Ms/2}s^{−3/2}ds. Since e^{−sK(q)}=e^{−2Js}exp(2Js cos q), for r≥1

j̃_r=sqrt(M/(2π))∫_0^∞e^{−(M/2+2J)s} I_r(2Js)s^{−3/2}ds>0.

This proves all coefficients positive, not only 12 computed ones. ## 4. ВАЖНО: continuum locality is a separate limit (R133)

Fixed circumference C, a=C/N; fixed *index* range R gives physical d=Ra→0, while fixed d implies R grows with N. Even fixed R requires appropriate scaling of J_r, stability and a limit of the observable; moment order alone does not prove continuum convergence. Distinguish local continuum Schrödinger, finite-dimensional regulator/physical finite ring, and continuum with nonlocal jumps. Counterexample on unit circle, P_phi eigenvalues 2πn+phi, H_phi=P_phi²+2g(1−cos(P_phi/2)), g fixed. For phi=π cos((2πn+π)/2)=0; for 0 cos(πn)=(−1)^n. With β=1/T, Z_pi=e^{−2gβ}Z_pi^free, Z0=e^{−2gβ}[cosh(2gβ)Z0^free+sinh(2gβ)Σ_n(−1)^n exp(−β(2πn)²)]. Poisson: free twist difference exponentially small, alternating trace suppressed exp(−T/16). Thus C_free=1−sech(2g/T)+exponentially small corrections ~2g²/T², despite continuum Hilbert space. g=1 t²C at T=200,500,1000,2000: 2.00289774,1.99998667,1.99999667,1.99999917. Nonlocal physical half-circumference jump survives. No universal power-law exclusion in continuum; no general violation of diamagnetic inequality claimed. For full local real scalar minimally coupled Schrödinger heat kernel, C≥0 remains; projection may be gauge covariant yet fail positivity/diamagnetic control. A negative measured C with purported full scalar assumptions flags mismatch, preparation or systematic error; not automatic proof of a novel mechanism.
